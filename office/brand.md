## Voice and tone

- Plain, confident, warm. Speaks like a wise mentor at the kitchen table, not a lecture hall.
- Short sentences. One idea at a time.
- Respectful of every viewer, including those who disagree.

## Visual style (starting point)

- Dark, rich backgrounds with gold accents (royal, "kingdom" feel).
- Thumbnails: one strong image plus a maximum of 4 words of bold text.
- Clean captions on every Short (most people watch muted).
